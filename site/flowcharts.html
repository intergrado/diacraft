DiaCraft/Flowcharts

A free flowchart maker for process flows.

Draw a flowchart or a process flow from the Basic Flowchart shapes, join the steps with connectors that stay glued as you move them, and lay the page out again in one step. Swimlanes show who does each step.

Start from a template

Choose File → New and pick a template. Basic Flowchart opens a portrait page with the flowchart shapes beside the basic shapes, in the quiet green theme Fernwood. Cross-Functional Flowchart opens a landscape page with three swimlanes and three phases already in place. A blank drawing opens seven of the built-in stencils in the Shapes window, and More Shapes opens the others. For a business process in BPMN, the BPMN Diagram template opens the BPMN shapes: tasks, gateways, events, pools and lanes, and their flows.

The flowchart shapes

The Basic Flowchart stencil has 18 shapes: Process, Decision, Terminator, Data, Document, Multiple Documents, Predefined Process, Manual Input, Manual Operation, Preparation, On-Page Reference, Off-Page Reference, Database, Internal Storage, Delay, Display, Summing Junction and Or. Drag one onto the page, or click it to drop it in the middle of the view. Double-click a shape, or select it and start typing, to give it a label.

To find a shape in any stencil, type in the Search shapes box and press Enter. The shapes you use most can go at the top of a stencil as Quick Shapes.

Connect the steps

  • Draw a connector. Choose the Connector tool (Home → Tools, or Ctrl+3) and drag from one shape to the next.
  • Use AutoConnect. Rest the pointer on a shape and four arrows appear. Click one to join the nearest shape in that direction, or to add a copy of the shape and join it.
  • Move a shape. The connectors glued to it follow.
  • Choose the line. On the Design tab, Layout → Connectors sets right-angle, straight or curved lines, and Show Line Jumps draws a jump where two connectors cross.
  • Adjust a route. Drag the handle in the middle of a leg of a right-angle connector to move that leg. Right-click a connector to reset it or to choose how it reroutes.
  • Label a branch. Give a connector a label, such as Yes or No after a decision, and choose its arrowheads from 46 line ends.

Swimlanes and cross-functional flowcharts

A cross-functional flowchart shows who does each step. The Cross-Functional Flowchart template starts with three lanes, one for each person or team, and three phases across them. Drop a flowchart shape into a lane and it moves with the lane. The Cross-Functional Flowchart tab adds lanes and separators, turns the lanes and rotates their labels.

Lay a flowchart out automatically

Design → Layout → Re-Layout Page lays the page out again in one step: as a flowchart from top to bottom, left to right, bottom to top or right to left, or as a hierarchy, a compact tree, a radial or a circular layout. It moves the shapes, routes the connectors again, and Undo puts everything back. More Layout Options sets the spacing and the style of the connectors.

To space shapes evenly without a new layout, select them and choose Home → Arrange → Position → Auto Space. Each column and row of shapes keeps its alignment, and the connectors follow.

Share the flowchart

Save the drawing as .diacraft, .vsdx or .odg. Export a page as PNG, SVG or PDF from File → Export, or print it with a preview of the sheets. To put the same title block on several pages, draw it on a background page and assign that page to them.
