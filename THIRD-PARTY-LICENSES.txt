DiaCraft: third-party licences
================================================================================

DiaCraft is free software under the MIT License or the Apache License 2.0, at your option
(LICENSE-MIT, LICENSE-APACHE, NOTICE). Its programs, `diacraft` and `diacraft-cli`, are built
from the Rust crates listed below, and from the Rust standard library. Each crate has its own
licence, and the licence asks that its notice and text go with the programs. This file is that
notice: it lists every crate the programs are built from, on each platform, and gives each
licence text once, with the crates that carry it. One crate, `libmimalloc-sys`, also compiles a
C library into the programs; its entry says so. The two fonts and the pictures DiaCraft carries
are in NOTICE and ATTRIBUTION.md, which come in the download too.

This file is made by `cargo xtask licenses` from Cargo.lock and the crates' own files, and
`cargo xtask licenses --check` keeps it in step. Do not edit it by hand.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. What is in it
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Linux                  239 crates (diacraft 239, diacraft-cli 86), 9 licences, 123 texts
  Windows                186 crates (diacraft 186, diacraft-cli 85), 9 licences, 103 texts
  macOS (Apple silicon)  179 crates (diacraft 179, diacraft-cli 88), 9 licences, 102 texts
  macOS (Intel)          179 crates (diacraft 179, diacraft-cli 88), 9 licences, 102 texts

The counts leave out DiaCraft's own 24 crates, which are under DiaCraft's licence. A crate that
offers a choice of licences (MIT OR Apache-2.0) is listed under the one DiaCraft uses it under:
MIT where it is offered, then Apache-2.0. The programs also use the computer's own libraries
(its C library, the window system and the graphics drivers), which are not part of the download.
