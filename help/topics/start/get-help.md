When a topic does not answer your question, there are three places to look, in this order.

## Search the help {#search}

Search the help for the words you would use: the keywords of a topic hold the words people try, not only the words the topic itself uses. See [Use help](topic:use-help) for how search and links work.

## Look at the public issues {#issues}

DiaCraft's problems and requests are kept as issues on its public repository. Look through the [open issues](https://github.com/intergrado/diacraft/issues) for yours: someone may have met it, and the discussion there may hold an answer.

## Report a problem or a request {#report}

If nobody has reported it, [open a new issue](https://github.com/intergrado/diacraft/issues/new). Say which version of DiaCraft you use, on which system, what you did, what you expected and what happened. A small file that shows the problem helps, and so does the text of any error message.

> A security problem is not reported in a public issue. Use the private route that the [security policy](https://github.com/intergrado/diacraft/security/policy) describes.
