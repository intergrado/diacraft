This is DiaCraft's help. It is part of the program, so it works with no network connection, and it opens beside your drawing.

The sections of the help home list the topics, grouped by what you are doing. Choose a topic to read it.

[Use help](topic:use-help) says how to move around in help and what its links do. [Get help](topic:get-help) says where to turn when a topic does not answer your question.
