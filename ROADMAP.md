# DiaCraft roadmap

**Stage: an early release (0.2.3)** · next: 0.3, in which an `.odg` drawing comes back from `.diacraft` with the same content and a drawing is checked before a save (the Compatibility Checker), while the M1 to M5 slices keep running. No hours are given until M1 sets the parity count.

> **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-07

DiaCraft targets behavioural parity with the reference app (the desktop diagramming application whose files DiaCraft opens and saves), and adds what the reference app lacks: Linux, macOS, FreeBSD and web builds, agent control through a CLI and an MCP server, and a free licence. The reference app was not inspected, nothing was launched or measured, and our output has not been opened in it by the project.

**Where 0.2 stands:** the desktop window opens `.vsdx` files and OpenDocument drawings (`.odg`, `.fodg`) and draws them, and edits and saves drawings: shapes from nine built-in stencils (Basic Shapes, Basic Flowchart, Decorative Shapes, Arrow Shapes, Gane-Sarson, the Cross-Functional Flowchart Shapes, the Organization Chart Shapes, Network Devices and Computers and Devices) and from opened stencil files, connectors that stay glued and reroute, text with formatting, fill, line, font and paragraph formatting, rotate, flip, align, order, copy, paste, group, undo and redo, and save and reopen; a shape's ShapeSheet is read and edited in a window of its own, and any cell, row or section of it is set by a command. Much of the target app is still missing (the parity checklist scores 36% weighted breadth), and nothing is checked against the target app itself.

## Upcoming

The milestones are in [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md). 0.2.0 was the first public release, with DiaCraft's own `.diacraft` format as the default (its row there says what it holds); 0.2.1 renamed the download files and 0.2.2 added the MCP bundles; 0.2.3, the version on `main`, adds the ShapeSheet window and the commands that set any cell; and the next milestone is 0.3 ([`docs/gaps.md`](docs/gaps.md) says what is left for it); the M1 baseline and the M2 to M5 slices keep running beside it. After the 0.3 items, four features are scheduled next, in the order the owner listed them (2026-11-25), and all four are under way: connectors that route around shapes and are routed again when a shape moves (designed and approved; the router crate, the page's routing settings and their tab, and the obstacle rules are built, and the router does not route the engine's connectors yet); the ShapeSheet window, where a shape's formula cells are shown and edited, with a command that sets any cell, which the command line and the MCP server reach too (the window reads a sheet and edits its cells, and the commands that set cells and add and delete rows and sections are built; the window's rows and sections are next); opening draw.io files (`.drawio`) and their compressed form (designed; the reader's frame, its cells, its styles and its labels are built, and its shapes are next); and more stencils of DiaCraft's own (UML, BPMN, C4, network and database), drawn by the project with no copied art (the network and computers stencils are built). The Current focus of [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md#current-focus) lists them. Beyond the reference app, the owner wants AI agents inside the app, talking to a person in a chat pane and drawing or editing through the app's commands; it is listed under Additions beyond the reference app in the same file, not yet designed or scheduled. Engines shared with the other Craft apps are carved into craft-libs in the order listed there under Shared libraries: path operations (in review), snapping, the bounded package layer, metafiles and SVG, ODF, then DXF, effects and the Office package layer.
